# Zomato Sales Performance Dashboard

An interactive Excel dashboard developed to analyze Zomato sales performance across multiple cities, food categories, customer ratings, and geographical regions.

The dashboard combines KPI monitoring, trend analysis, category-wise performance tracking, and state-level sales visualization through dynamic filters and interactive charts.

## Project Structure

The workbook consists of three interconnected sheets:

### Dashboard
The main reporting page that presents key metrics, charts, maps, and business insights in a single view. Users can interact with slicers to explore different segments of the data.

### Pivot Data
Contains all pivot tables and calculations used to power the dashboard visuals and KPI cards.

### Zomato Data
The source dataset containing order details, customer ratings, locations, food categories, and sales information used throughout the project.

---
## KPI Overview

The dashboard presents five key business metrics that provide a quick snapshot of overall sales performance. All KPI cards update dynamically based on the selected filters, enabling focused analysis across different cities, categories, and time periods.

<img width="1144" height="132" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8d6e9b95-9df6-4116-a785-6c64b4aae977" />

| KPI | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Total Sales | Total revenue generated from all selected orders |
| Average Rating | Average customer rating across the filtered records |
| Rating Count | Total number of ratings received |
| Total Orders | Total orders included in the selected dataset |
| Average Order Value | Average revenue earned per order |

### Current Dashboard Summary

Based on the complete dataset:

- **Total Sales:** ₹53.01M
- **Average Rating:** 4.34
- **Rating Count:** 5.59M
- **Total Orders:** 197.43K
- **Average Order Value:** ₹268.51

These metrics serve as the foundation for evaluating sales performance, customer satisfaction, and order behavior across the platform.

- Quarterly analysis shows Q2 generated the highest revenue among all quarters.

## Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes dynamic slicers that allow users to explore sales performance from different perspectives. All charts, maps, and KPI cards update instantly based on the selected filters.

<img width="294" height="301" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/5eecdb47-0741-4513-bc2c-9cb1fc623546" />

### Month Filter
Allows users to analyze sales performance for specific months or compare multiple months simultaneously. This helps identify seasonal trends and revenue fluctuations throughout the year.

### City Filter
Enables city-level analysis by filtering the dashboard to specific locations. Users can compare sales performance across different cities and identify top-performing markets.

### Dynamic Cross-Filtering
All dashboard visuals are interconnected. Any filter selection automatically updates:

- KPI Cards
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Daily Sales Trend
- Weekly Sales Trend
- Quarterly Performance Analysis
- Sales by Food Type
- Top 5 Cities by Sales
- State-wise Sales Map

This interactive filtering system allows quick exploration of business performance across different regions and time periods.

## Key Business Insights
<img width="295" height="282" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/db21e1c9-23dd-4118-ab54-135cd9c956d9" />

### Top Performing State
- Karnataka generated the highest sales revenue of **₹5.46M**, contributing approximately **10.3%** of total sales.

### Food Category Performance
- Vegetarian food items dominated revenue generation, contributing **₹34.18M** (64% of total sales).
- Non-Vegetarian food items generated **₹18.83M** in sales.

### Peak Sales Day
- Saturday recorded the highest revenue at **₹7.78M**, making it the strongest performing day of the week.

### Lowest Sales Day
- Tuesday generated the lowest revenue at **₹7.36M**, indicating comparatively lower customer activity.

### Revenue Distribution
- Sales performance was concentrated among a few major cities and states, with Bengaluru and Karnataka emerging as key revenue drivers.
